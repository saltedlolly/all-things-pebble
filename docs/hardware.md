## Using Your Pebble

- **Buttons.** Back is on the left. On the right are Up (top, opens Health by default), Select (middle, opens the menu) and Down (bottom, opens the Timeline).
- **Exit an app.** Press and hold Back.
- **Quiet Time.** On the watchface, long-press Back to toggle Quiet Time (by default).
- **Hard reset.** Hold Back, Up and Select together for about 30 seconds, until a progress bar appears.
- [PebbleOS settings notes](https://github.com/clach04/not-yet-definitive-pebble-guide/blob/main/settings.md) - Notes on the on-watch settings in PebbleOS.
